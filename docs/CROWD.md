# The crowd: readers like you

Booked is about people you trust. The crowd adds readers you have not met whose taste matches yours, as either a person to follow or a book they loved. Designs: the "The crowd" section of the design canvas (boards 31 to 32d).

## Four variants

| | One card | A band |
|---|---|---|
| **People** | "Follow Greg": you and Greg, the match, the evidence. On Home. | "Readers like you": avatars with match and genre, with See all. In Discover and between Feed posts. |
| **Books** | "Loved by readers like you": one book, who loved it, their match. On Home. | Covers with the matching reader under each. In Discover. |

## Rules

1. **Friends first.** On Home the friends' picks rank above the crowd. Crowd cards are blue and labeled "Readers like you", never orange.
2. **Zero friends: invite first, crowd below.** Order: stories band, Build your circle, Reader Identity, one crowd card.
3. **Follow, then wait for follow back.** A followed reader joins the circle, and counts toward the five friends, only once they follow back. Until then the card says "Waiting for Greg", with Undo.
4. **Match is a number plus evidence.** Percentage and one line of proof (books in common, one book you both loved). A match needs at least 8 books in common; under 8 scanned books, no people are shown.
5. **Opt-in to be found.** Off by default. Others see first name, photo, top genres and match, plus ratings only if shared. The library stays private.
6. **Free and Premium.** Seeing suggestions and following are free. Premium: comparing two Reader Identities side by side, and seeing everyone beyond your circle who loved a book.
7. **Home is short.** At most one crowd card on Home, dismissable, not shown again for 3 days.
